## **1 Introduction** 

This document describes a way to demonstrate compliance with the essential cyber resilience requirements of the EU Regulation 2024/2847 "Cyber Resilience Act" (CRA) using an ISO 27001 compliant ISMS. 

This document will rene and extend the requirements of the ISO 27001 to include the design, development or production as well as vulnerability handling of products with digital elements (PwDE) as well as part thereof subject to the CRA. 

This document is NOT a new management system standard independent of ISO/IEC 27001, but rather 

- a. species CRA-specic requirements that are composed of renements of and additions to requirements in ISO/IEC 27001 based on the essential cybersecurity requirements of the Cyber Resilience Act as set out in Annex A. 

- b. species CRA-specic guidance that supports additions to and/or modications of ISO/IEC 27002 (see Clause 6).

## **2 Scope** 

The scope of this document is the application of the requirements of ISO 27001:2022 and additional requirements for products under the Scope of the CRA. 

This document addresses organizations developing or manufacturing PwDE or having PwDE designed, developed or manufactured, and marketing them under their name or trademark, i.e. the manufacturer (CRA Article 3 Point 13). This applies even if the product is not directly placed on the European market by the manufacturer itself. 

As show in Figure 1, the scope of the ISMS includes the business services for placing a PwDE on the market as well as the business services for providing a remote data processing solutions (RPDS) as part of the PwDE. 

**Note:** The specic denition of RDPS belonging to a PwDE relies on additional guidance by the European Commission. For the meantime this guideline will assume that RDPS entails at least software designed, developed and operated by or on behalf of the manufacturer, whose absence would prevent the product with digital elements from performing one of its function. (Article 3 Point 2 CRA)

**Note:** Generally, the RDPS is a part of the PwDE and therefore subject to the same requirements under the CRA. For the sake of clarity, this document differentiates between the two as the RDPS stays under the control of the manufacturer and the PwDE placed on the market is operated by a third party user. Preferably both parts follow the same processes. 

Both of the above mentioned services include design, development or production as well as vulnerability handling processes in relation to their respective output.

## **4 Terms and denitions** 

For the purposes of this document, the terms and denitions given in ISO/IEC 27000 as well as the denitions in Article 3 of the CRA apply. 

Additional denitions used in this document: 

## Affected third-party 

User, society and other third-parties potentially adversely affected by a cybersecurity risk of the PwDE in scope. 

Compliant specication 

Normative documents specifying the essential cybersecurity requirements of the CRA which are compliant to assumptions set out in this document and usable in the scope of module H. 

Design, development and production process 

Processes of the manufacturer resulting in placing a PwDE on the market or performing modications to product afterwards including all relevant activities from initial concept to nal product as well as quality assurance processes. 

## PwDE in scope 

PwDE, type of PwDE or multiple types of PwDE developed or manufactured by the manufacturer or designed, developed or manufactured on behalf of the manufacturer, and marketed under the name or trademark of the manufacturer, which is subject to the processes set out in this document to demonstrate conformity. 

## Vulnerability handling process 

The process of identifying, evaluating, treating and disclosing vulnerabilities in a PwDE during its dened support period. Treatment includes mitigation or elimination of the vulnerability through appropriate measures such as patches, updates or workarounds and might result in a modication of the PwDE via the design, development and production process.

## **5 CRA-specic requirements related to ISO/IEC 27001:2022** 

## **5.1 General** 

All requirements from ISO/IEC 27001:2022, Clauses 4 to 10, that do not appear below shall apply unchanged. 

## **5.2 Applicability of cybersecurity controls for products** 

This document refers to compliant specications for specic product types and use cases. These specications rene the essential cybersecurity requirements set out in CRA Annex I or part there of and follow certain assumptions given in this document. 

Compliant specications are 

1. Preferably harmonised European standards (hEN) cited for presumption of conformity in their respective scope 

2. Otherwise the Technical Guideline BSI TR-03183-1 including compliant proles if no appropriate hEN exists or the appropriate hEN does not full the assumptions set out in this guideline 

Essential cybersecurity requirements or part there of should not be interpreted directly without compliant specication. 

## **5.3 CRA 4.2 Understanding the needs and expectations of interested parties** 

ISO/IEC 27001:2022, 4.2 is rened as follows. 

1. Interested parties that are relevant to the information security management system shall at least include the manufacturer and affected third-parties; 

2. Relevant requirements of these interested parties, include at least the requirement for the manufacturer for compliance with the CRA and the affected third-parties expectations regarding an appropriate level of cybersecurity based the intended purpose and reasonably foreseeable use of the PwDE in Scope; 

3. Requirements addressed through the information security management system include at least the requirement of the manufacturer for compliance with the CRA and the affected thirdparties expectations regarding an appropriate level of cybersecurity for the intended purpose and reasonably foreseeable use of the PwDE in Scope; 

## **5.4 CRA 4.3 Determining the scope of the information security management system** 

ISO/IEC 27001:2022, 4.2 is rened as follows.

The scope of the information security management system must include at least the PwDE in Scope to be covered by the ISMS as well as the following services related to the PwDE in Scope: 

1. Placing a PwDE on the market consisting of: 

   - a. The design, development and production process of the PwDE in scope

## **5.5 CRA 5.2 Policy** 

ISO/IEC 27001:2022, 5.2 is rened as follows. 

1. information security objectives shall include at least 

   - a. that the PwDE in scope ensures an appropriate level of cybersecurity based on the risks as set out in Annex I Part I CRA. 

   - b. that the manufacturer fulls the vulnerability handling requirements as set out in Annex I Part II CRA during the dened support period of PwDE in scope. 

## **5.6 CRA 6.1.2 Information security risk assessment** 

ISO/IEC 27001:2022, 6.1.2. is rened as follows. 

The risk acceptance criteria as set out in 6.1.2. a) 1) shall include the following criteria: 

1. Non-compliance with the CRA is not acceptable 

2. Risks to affected third-parties can only be accepted if an appropriate level of cybersecurity based on the intended purpose and reasonably foreseeable use of the PwDE in scope is ensured. 

**Assumption:** This document assumes that a compliant specication denes, at least implicitly, appropriate acceptance criteria for risks of affected third-parties in their respective scope. If a harmonized European Standard (hEN) is cited for presumption of conformity it is assumed that the regulator accepts that all residual risks not addressed can be accepted for the scope of the hES until further notice. TR-03183-1 denes general risk acceptance criteria and applies as described in Annex B. 

The identied risks as set out in 6.1.2. c) shall include at least:

## **5.7 CRA 6.1.3 Information security risk treatment** 

In addition to ISO/IEC 27001, 6.1.3 the following CRA-specic requirements apply: 

6.1.3 a-1) In addition to 6.1.3. a) the information security risk treatment options shall include a secure development lifecycle as part of the design, development and production process ensuring an appropriate level of cybersecurity based on the risks assessed in 6.1.2 in order to full the applicable requirements of Annex A.1. 

6.1.3 a-2) In addition to 6.1.3. a) the information security risk treatment options shall include management of technical vulnerabilities as part of the vulnerability handling process of the manufacturer in order to full all requirements of Annex A.2. 

6.1.3 c-1) In addition to 6.1.3. c) shall compare the controls of the PwDE in scope implemented as part of the secure development lifecycle in 6.1.3 a-1) above with those in Annex A.1 and based on the risk verify that no necessary controls have been omitted. 

6.1.3 d-1) The produced statement of applicability shall also include appropriate risk based justication for omitting any controls in Annex A.1 during the secure development lifecycle of the PwDE in scope based on the risk acceptance criteria dened in 6.1.2 a) above. 

**Assumption:** This document assumes that a compliant specication for Annex I Part I CRA renes applicable essential cybersecurity requirements with specic controls and potentially excludes some essential cybersecurity requirements using a risk-based justication. TR-03183-1 applies as described in Annex B.

7.5.1-3) Documented information as required by 7.5.1-2 and 7.5.1-3 shall be truthful, correct, current and sufciently complete to monitor and evaluate the design, development and production process of the PwDE in scope as well as the vulnerability handling processes of the manufacturer to demonstrate CRA compliance. 

## **5.10 CRA 8.1 Operational planning and control** 

ISO/IEC 27001:2022, 7.4. is rened as follows. 

The criteria for the design, development and production process of the PwDE shall include that the secure development lifecycle as dened in 6.1.3 b-1) are implemented or otherwise realized as designed and appropriately veried based on the associated risk before placing a PwDE on the market. 

The criteria for the vulnerability handling process of the manufacturer includes that the controls as dened in 6.1.3 b-2) are implemented by the manufacturer as part of the vulnerability handling process during the dened support period of the PwDE in scope. 

**Assumption:** This document assumes that any compliant specication of Annex I Part I CRA is either concise enough or gives additional guidance to verify that controls determined in 6.1.3 b-1) are properly implemented by the PwDE in scope. Furthermore this document assumes that any compliant specication for Annex II CRA is either concise enough or gives additional guidance to verify that the controls determined in 6.1.3. b-2) are provided by the manufacturer as part of the vulnerability handling process. TR-03183-1 applies as described in Annex B.

## **8 Annex B: Application of TR-03183-1** 

The TR-03183-1 is applied using this rationale: 

1. TR-03183-1 provides a comprehensive set of requirements for manufacturers and products that can be used as a basis for the risk assessment and risk treatment and risk treatment required in 6.1.2 and 6.1.3 of this document. Applicable risk acceptance and evaluation criteria based on the intended purpose and foreseeable use of the PwDE are set out in TR-03183-1 5.14. 

2. The controls of TR-03183-1 TR-03183-1 Chapter 8 and Chapter 9 are derived for the essential cybersecurity requirements set out in Annex A.1 and A.2 of this document. The application of the controls is performed by ltering the provided control set according to the risk to generate a baseline for the PwDE and an applicability statement of essential cybersecurity requirements set out in 6.1.3 d-1 of this document. 

3. TR-03183-1 Chapter 10 renes the requirements of Annex II CRA for user information and can be used to generate appropriate user documentation as set out in 7.5. 

4. The TR-03183-1 RH_RT.1.3 establishes requirements for verication of implemented controls in the product design and development process that can be applied to full the requirements of 8.1 of this document.
