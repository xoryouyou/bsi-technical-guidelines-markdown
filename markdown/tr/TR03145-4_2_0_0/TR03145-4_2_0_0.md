1 Introduction 

## 1 Introduction 

This document is Part 4 of the Technical Guideline series (BSI-TR-03145) and defines requirements for the operation of a Certification Authority with security level 'high'. The document series compiles requirements for a Certification Authority (CA) in a Public Key infrastructure (PKI) with security level 'high' according to Technical Guideline  (BSI-TR-03107). 

Part 1 considers generic requirements for secure operation of a CA with security level 'high'. The other parts of the series consider additional or modified requirements for specific business cases of PKI. 

This document, part 4 of (BSI-TR-03145), defines requirements being specific for the secure operation of the Public Key infrastructure for the Extended Access Control of the German Official Travel Documents according to (BSI-TR-03110)[1] . 

## 1.1 Scope and structure of this document 

This document concerns the PKI for the Extended Access Control of the German Official Travel Documents, named CVCA-PKI in the following. The CVCA consists of a root-CA, called Country Verifying Certificate Authority (CVCA) and one or more sub-CAs, called Document Verifier (DV). 

This document is Part 4 of (BSI-TR-03145) and contains necessary refinements and additions for the specific needs of all Certification Authorities in the CVCA-PKI. In order to be compliant to this document all Certification Authorities in the CVCA-PKI SHALL fulfil all requirements of part 1 of (BSI-TR-03145) which are not refined or discarded here in Part 4 and the additional requirements of Part 4. 

In this document the general term CA is used if a requirement applies to both, CVCA and DVs. If requirements do not apply to both or might differ, it will be mentioned explicitly. Furthermore, the requirements in this document apply to CAs in the PKI for the German eID application (CVCA-eID PKI), the German ePass application (CVCA-ePass PKI) and the German eSign application (CVCA-eSign PKI). If requirements do not apply to all these applications or might differ, it will be mentioned explicitly. There are two types of certificates in the CVCA-eID PKI: Card Verifiable certificates (CV certificates) and Meta Data Signer certificates (MDS certificates). If not stated otherwise, the requirements in this document apply to both, CV and MDS certificates. 

Note: This document reflects requirements of the root-CA’s Certificate Policies of the CVCA-PKI[2] in order to enable conformance to part 4 of (BSI-TR-03145) and the mentioned Certificate Policies as well, but it does neither replace nor comprise entirely the requirements of the Certificate Policies or regulations by law. 

This document contains three types of requirements: 

- requirements from (BSI-TR-03145-1) refined for application in the CVCA-PKI; 

- requirements from (BSI-TR-03145-1) which are not applicable in the CVCA-PKI; 

- requirements in addition to (BSI-TR-03145-1) to be used in the CVCA-PKI. 

Annex 5 contains additional requirements which only concern and are normative to those DVs issuing certificates for the eID application of the German eID scheme. Those DVs are called “BerCAs”.

## 3.1 CA key life cycle management (Section 3.1 of (BSI-TR-03145-1)) 

The following requirement from (BSI-TR-03145-1) concerning the key life cycle management of the CA’s own keys is refined as follows: 

- **3.1A1** Private keys of the CA shall be generated, held, used and deleted in a security device following the requirements of the Certificate Policy of the root-CA ensuring the claimed security features, including tamper resistance, mitigation of side channels, suitable access control, random number generator and security of cryptographic operations. 

## **3.1.1 CA key generation (Section 3.1.1 of (BSI-TR-03145-1))** 

According to (BSI-TR-03145-1) control procedure (3) from Section 7.13.1 of (ISO27099) SHALL be fulfilled as requirement. This requirement concerning the generation of the CA’s own key pair is refined as stated below: 

- **3.1.1R1** The CA key pair generation SHALL be performed by trustworthy personnel during a key ceremony in a four-eyes principle under the attendance of the key responsible officer in a physically secured environment. 

The following requirement concerning the generation of the CA’s own keys is refined as follows: 

- **3.1.1A1** The generation of a new key pair and the corresponding CA certificate SHALL be done according to the deadlines defined in the corresponding CP. 

The following requirement does not apply to CAs affected by this document: 

- control procedure (5) from Section 7.13.1 of (ISO27099) 

## **3.1.2 CA key storage, backup, and recovery (Section 3.1.2 of (BSI-TR-03145-1))** 

The following requirement concerning the storage, backup and recovery of the CA’s own keys is refined as follows: 

- **3.1.2A1** The root-CA SHALL make backups of its keys. Sub-CAs (in case of the CVCA-eID this includes DVs and Terminals) MAY make backups of their keys. The requirements defined in (KLSR) for the corresponding security level SHALL be fulfilled.

## 4 Certificate life cycle management 

## 4.1 Subject registration (Section 4.1 of (BSI-TR-03145-1)) 

The following requirement concerning the subject registration is refined as follows: 

- **4.1** R **6** The CA SHALL ensure, that the requesting subject is identified unambiguously before starting the certificate generation process. The CA SHALL verify certificate requests as specified in the corresponding CP. In particular, the CA SHALL validate the signatures on certificate requests for successive certificate. 

## 4.2 Certificate renewal (not supported) (Section 4.2 of (BSI-TR03145-1)) 

The renewal of a certificate is defined as extending the validity period of a certificate. The public key of the subscriber and the subscriber information of the certificate are not modified. Renewal of certificates is not allowed within the CVCA PKI. Therefore, all requirements referenced or contained in Section 4.2 of (BSI-TR03145-1) do not apply to CAs affected by this document. 

## 4.3 Certificate rekey (Section 4.3 of (BSI-TR-03145-1)) 

Rekeying of a certificate is defined as issuing a new certificate based on a newly generated subscriber key pair and a new validity period. The remaining content of the certificate (i.e. the attributes of the subscriber) stays usually unchanged. 

The CVCA CPs of the CVCA-PKI allow rekeying of a certificate based on a rekeying request with a newly generated key pair. Note, that this does not apply to Metadata Signer Certificates (MDS certificates) within the CVCA-eID PKI. MDS certificates are not rekeyed. A new MDS certificate for this subject shall always be requested following the process of initial application. Therefore, the requirements in this section do not apply to MDS certificates and their certificate lifecycle management. 

The following requirement from (BSI-TR-03145-1) concerning the rekeying of certificates is refined as follows: 

- **4.3A1** For the case of expiring certificates, the CA SHALL define in the CP and/or CPS that a request for the rekeying of a certificate SHALL be submitted according to the time frames defined in the CA’s CP. The CA SHALL be able to generate the new certificate within the defined timeframe.

## 4.7 Certificate revocation (Section 4.7 of (BSI-TR-03145-1)) 

The requirements in this section concern the revocation of certificates. Note, that within the CVCA-eID PKI only MDS certificates are revoked and only the revocation of MDS certificates is published by means of Certificate Revocation Lists. Therefore, each CA within the CVCA-eID PKI shall provide a revocation service for MDS certificates and shall maintain the revocation status information of MDS certificates. CV certificates within the CVCA-eID are not revoked, incidents are handled via short-term end-user certificates and the suspension of subscribers (see 4.8). Thus, the requirements in this section apply to MDS certificates within the CVCA-eID PKI only. 

Control procedure (10) from section 7.15.6 of (ISO27099), which is referenced in (BSI-TR-03145-1), is refined as follows: 

- **4.7R1** The CA SHALL maintain controls to revoke MDS certificates and publish appropriate information about revoked MDS certificates. 

Additionally, the following requirements apply: 

- **4.7A10** Revocation lists of MDS certificates SHALL be backed up to a physically separated server to avoid the loss of data. 

- **4.7A11** The integrity of the revocation information SHALL be secured by appropriate means. 

- **4.7A12** The service processing the revocation of certificates SHALL ensure the processing of inquiries and updates as specified in the CA’s CP. 

- **4.7A13** The CA SHOULD document and archive each revocation request and actually performed revocation in a comprehensible way. This documentation and archiving SHOULD include at least: 

   - entity that applied for revocation of a certificate, 

   - name of revoked subject if present and known at time of revocation, 

   - serial number of revoked certificate if present and known at time of revocation, 

   - reasonable evidence for the legitimacy of the revoking party, 

   - date and time of revocation, 

   - date and time of invalidity, and 

   - revocation reason.

5 Annex: Revocation Service for the eID application 

## 5 Annex: Revocation Service for the eID application[3] 

The Revocation Service for the eID application is a process to prevent the misuse of the eID application with, for example, lost or stolen identity cards or residence permits. Therefore, it only concerns BerCAs. 

For details on the Revocation Service for the eID application please refer to (BSI TR-03127). 

For BerCAs, the following requirements apply in addition to those of (BSI-TR-03145-1): 

- REVeID.Req.1a The BerCA SHALL install appropriate technical and organizational measure to ensure the confidentiality of the revocation token at all times. 

- REVeID.Req.2a The BerCA SHALL generate the key pairs for the revocation lists only within a security area fulfilling the following criteria: separated area, access control, access only for authorized personnel, (ISO27001) certificate covering the security area and the generation and use of the key pairs for the revocation lists. 

- REVeID.Req.3a The BerCA SHALL delete the outdated eID revocation list. 

- REVeID.Req.4a The BerCA SHALL compute its service specific revocation list based on DER encoding according to (ITU-T X.690).
